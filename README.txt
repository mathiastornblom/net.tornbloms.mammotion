Control and monitor your Mammotion robot mower (Luba 2, Luba 3) from Homey. Start, pause, stop, or send your mower back to the dock, and adjust blade height and cutting speed on the fly. Toggle the headlamp, side LED and rain protection right from your device tile or a Flow.

Important: Mammotion only allows one active login per account, so connecting your main account directly would sign you out of the mobile app. Use a free second Mammotion account for Homey instead — the setup wizard walks you through creating one and sharing your mower to it. This takes about 2 minutes and only needs to be done once.

Keep an eye on battery level, charge cycles, blade wear, mowed area and progress, WiFi/Bluetooth signal, GPS satellite count and RTK positioning accuracy — all reported live and tracked in Insights.

Connects over the cloud by default, and switches to a direct Bluetooth connection automatically when your mower is in range, for faster and more local control. Use Flow cards to start a mow with a custom blade height and speed, get notified when mowing starts or finishes, or react to low battery and error states.

Run the tasks you have already set up in the Mammotion app straight from a Flow with "Start mowing task" — same zones, cutting height, speed and angle as in the app — or mow one named zone with "Start mowing zone". "Mower finished a mowing job" fires the moment a job is done and tells you which task it was, so you can chain tasks back to back, and "Resume mowing" continues a paused job where it stopped. Tip: pause the task's schedule in the Mammotion app so it only runs when Homey starts it.

Privacy: if the app crashes, it can send an anonymous technical crash report to the developer to help fix the problem faster. No account, location or personal data is included, and this can be turned off in the app's settings.
